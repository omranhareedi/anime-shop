Our Origin
The Survey Corps Supply Depot was established within the walls of Akihabara — a hub for collectors and fans who refuse to live in cages. What started as a small squad of passionate otaku hunting for rare figures has grown into a curated division connecting humanity's finest with authentic, high-quality anime gear.
Wall Sina Standard
Our Promise
Every item in our depot is hand-selected from trusted vendors across Japan and beyond. From limited-edition 3D Maneuver Gear statues to streetwear that turns titans' heads — we bring the best of otaku culture within the walls. Authenticity isn't just our policy; it's our creed.
